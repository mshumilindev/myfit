# Spotter · Learn — video shot list

The complete how-to library for Spotter. **45 lessons** across **10 topics**, covering every part of the app that isn't behind a feature flag (Nutrition and gym-presence are excluded until they ship).

Every lesson is shot **twice** — a **Phone** cut (9:16, vertical, for watching on a phone) and a **Web** cut (16:9, for following along on desktop). Tick a box when that cut is recorded and uploaded. Titles and notes match `client/src/learn/catalog.ts` exactly (the in-app catalog), so the app and this list stay in sync.
