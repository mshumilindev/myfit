## Pass 2
- Real images: equipment photos (`img('equipment/…')`) in the kit card, review sheet, in-session tray, inventory tiles (Olympic barbell, Power bar, Technique bar,
  EZ curl bar, Axle bar, Bench press bar) and the selected-inventory sheet; names = equipmentCatalog.ts names of those ids.
- Gym images: GymThumb / ResultThumb / heroes use the app's final fallback **HouseGraphic** (graphite weave + centred barbell mark) — provider photos and
  OSM tiles are runtime-only.
- Band library: exact `BAND_HEX` swatches (loads.ts), picker shows all 10 colours.
- Muscle chips on inventory tiles = MuscleChip figure chips (kit bodymap crops).
- RouteMap = the app's StaticRouteMap drawing (roads, grid lines, brass route, green start, brass gym dot, "Route ready"/coords chip) instead of a hatched box.
- Hierarchy: 230 px full-bleed hero with 34 px name and a 54 px "Start session here"; today's hours as the big line in the hours card; stats as a flat strip
  (22–28 px values, no cards); gym cards 82 px with 56 px thumbs, results 64 px thumbs.
- Added: P11-Detail-Missing (stale gym link → back only), P11-Detail-Split-Hours (split ranges, website only, empty band library, inventory with 0 picked → no badge, counts only).
