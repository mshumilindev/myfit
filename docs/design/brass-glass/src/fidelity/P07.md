Hierarchy (rule #0b, from redesign.css / styles.css)
- Overview: `.ov-progress` spans two rows with a 40 px number and the 8-bar ramp → e1 hero; Trends tile turns danger-tinted when there are risks (`.ov-trends.is-risk`) → bad card; Program days 48 px cells, today accent; other tiles plain 112 px; Exercises row 72 px.
- Progress: `.pv-title` 30 px; `.kpi .big` 40 px + 11 px caps label → e1 hero (with the 140 px `.bars` on Total); section chips `.pv-chips` (pills, active accent); muscle rows, records and volume rows are chrome-less lists (`.record-row`, `.vol-row`); `.vol-note` / `.muscle-note` 12 px lines, not cards; DeloadCard accent (local) / danger (systemic); WeakPoints plain card.
- Trends: Atlas notes as bubbles with the portrait; insight cards plain, risk + attention tinted danger (left rule in the app), badges per level; hero numbers 28 px.
- Goals: Focus this block (live) = e1 hero with the painted FocusBodyMap; physique card e2 with the -lit silhouette; volume targets / suggested moves as flat rows.
- Mastery: `.mst-hero` (ring + rating 33 px + rank) = e1; Practice tile gold (`.mst-practice` loopwash) = e2; other axis tiles plain; CTA primary.
- Desktop: the selected record's chart pane = hero; volume map pane with the zone heatmap.

- Zones (volume.ts ZONE_COLOR): none neutral-800 · under neutral-600 · productive ok · high accent · over danger — bars, tags and heatmaps.
- Fatigue (fatigue.ts): fresh ok · moderate accent-600 (#b3833d) · high accent · fried danger.
- Readiness (recovery.ts): recovering danger · nearly accent · ready ok · stale kcal blue.

- Records: `record` tag ok green; record-group heads accent-100. Focus: grow accent, ease danger. Mastery status Good ok · Watch accent · Low danger.
