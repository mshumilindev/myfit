My Fit Gym discovery, imagery, and the live-session hero

Find the gym. Wear its picture.

Two additions to the system. Discovery: a gym is searched across databases first, then Google by name + location for a photo, and falls back to a house graphic that never looks broken. Presence: an open session becomes a photographic hero banner carried on every screen, phone and desktop, so the app is never ambiguous about being live. Ids are G-01 … G-12 (phone) and GW-01 … GW-06 (web).

Imagery rules
Resolution order — local DB → OpenStreetMap → Google Places → Foursquare → Google Images by “name + city gym” → house graphic. First hit with a photo over 600 px wins; the rest still merge into one venue row.

Storage — the chosen image is fetched once, downscaled to 1600 px, and cached on the user's own hub. Never hot-linked, never shown outside the app, replaceable from the gym menu.

Treatment — every photo carries a bottom-up scrim to 94% and sits behind text at ≥ 4.5:1. Photos appear at four sizes only: 320 px pane header, 210/150/60 px hero, 120 px card, 64 px row thumb.

House graphic — graphite weave plus the barbell mark, generated locally at any size. It is a valid final state, never labelled “missing”.
