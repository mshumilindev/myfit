My Fit Onboarding, avatars, and the admin / trainer roles

Sign-up as a first workout

The account form is replaced by a four-step arrival: who you are, your face, your gym, your first set. Each step owns the whole screen with a photographic ground, and the flow ends inside a live session rather than on a dashboard. Behind it, two new roles — admin creates people and hands out invite links; trainer reads the same data, but only for the clients an admin assigned. Ids: O-01 … O-10 onboarding, AD-01 … AD-07 admin, TR-01 … TR-04 trainer.

Rules · Nobody but the owner ever writes a logged set — a trainer who spots an error asks the member to fix it. A member's data is visible to exactly two roles: their admin, and the one trainer assigned to them. Revoking an assignment kills access on the next request, not at the next login. Every read of another person's data is written to an audit log the member can open from their profile.
